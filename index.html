💧 Intelligent Pump Hydrodynamics & Cavitation Twin

🎯 The Industrial Problem Solved

Centrifugal pumps in water supply, cooling towers, and district heating suffer from cavitation—the violent vapor bubble collapse that occurs when suction pressure drops below liquid vapor pressure (NPSHa < NPSHr). Cavitation erodes impeller blades, destroys mechanical seals, and causes pump failure. Furthermore, rapid valve shutoffs generate water hammer shockwaves (Joukowsky surge) that rupture pipelines.

💡 How This Digital Twin Solves It

Simulates pump Affinity Laws (Q proportional to n, H proportional to n^2), system resistance curves, Thoma cavitation parameters, and Motor Current Signature Analysis (MCSA) that detects cavitation non-intrusively via electrical stator current sidebands without expensive external vibration sensors.

📊 Telemetry & Control Glossary

  • NPSHa vs. NPSHr: Net Positive Suction Head Available (NPSHa) must exceed Net Positive Suction Head Required (NPSHr) to prevent fluid pressure from dropping below vapor pressure.
  • MCSA (Motor Current Signature Analysis): Non-intrusive electrical sensing method that detects mechanical cavitation torque ripple by monitoring stator current FFT sidebands (fe ± k*fr).
  • Joukowsky Water Hammer: Pressure surge equation (ΔP = ρ * a * Δv) describing shockwaves that propagate through piping when a fluid column is stopped instantaneously.
